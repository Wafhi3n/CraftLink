# Changelog - CraftLink

## v0.1.0 - 2026-11-24

First release. CraftLink is now an addon of its own: it shares your professions and the recipes you
know with your guild and friends, and Crafting Order players see you as a crafter without you
installing Crafting Order.

- Open each of your professions once. That's where CraftLink reads your recipes.
- `/craftlink` gives one status line. `/craftlink global` joins the global channel and
  `/craftlink global off` leaves it. Without that, CraftLink only talks to your guild and friends.
- With Crafting Order 1.53 or older installed, CraftLink pauses and leaves the sharing to it (it says
  so once).
- CraftLink doesn't take orders. To get them, install Crafting Order.
