## Summary

Shares your professions and the recipes you know with your guild and friends, so Crafting Order
players can find you without you installing Crafting Order.

## Description

Crafting Order helps players find someone who can craft what they need. CraftLink is the part that
tells them what you can make: your professions, their levels and the recipes you know. Install it on
its own if you want to be found and don't need the order window.

Open each of your professions once, the window that lists your recipes. That's where CraftLink reads
them, and it keeps them from then on, for each character. When you log in, it says hello to your
guildmates and friends who are online. It's an addon message, so nobody sees it in chat. Those who
run Crafting Order answer, and you show up in their crafters list with your recipes.

By default CraftLink only talks to your guild and your friends. Type /craftlink global to join the
global CraftLink channel, where players outside your guild can find you too, and /craftlink global
off to leave it. Your choice is remembered.

/craftlink on its own answers with one line: whether sharing is on, how many crafters you know, and
whether you're on the global channel. That's the only time CraftLink writes in your chat.

CraftLink doesn't take orders. A Crafting Order player can still send you one and you won't see it,
so install Crafting Order if you want to get orders. If you already run Crafting Order, CraftLink
stays paused and leaves the sharing to it (it tells you once).

WoW: Forever only. English, French, German and Spanish.
